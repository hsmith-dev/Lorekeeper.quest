- (event) 2026-11-01 E0-QA.15: x86 re-run of SPIKE-1, -2, -3 and the SPIKE-6 throughput harness on the remote desktop (Ryzen 9 9900X; CPU column = 4-core cpuset plus the AD-7 container limits; GPU column = RTX 5080). No target was changed.
- (pending) NFR-6 "Live caption lag", CPU: ≤ 30 s → **≤ 10 s**, and keep "sustained processing ≤ chunk duration". Evidence: faster-whisper base int8 on 4 cores processes a 15 s chunk in 0.43 s (worst 0.49 s). With the 7B generating on the same cores the worst is 2.5 s (RTF 0.18). 10 s leaves 4× headroom for a slower CPU and the socket path. The GPU target (≤ 5 s) is unchanged. E4 re-measures the end-to-end row before sign-off.
- (pending) NFR-6 "Narration of a 300-word recap", CPU: ≤ 2 min → **≤ 60 s**. Evidence: Kokoro-82M, one sentence per call, 316 words (105 s of audio) in 13.6 s on 4 cores (RTF 0.13). The GPU target (≤ 30 s) is unchanged (measured 0.49 s). **Condition:** the production tts image must first run within its AD-7 memory limit. The measured process peaked at 3.1 GB and was OOM-killed at 2.5 GB, so E5 / E0-Platform.10 measure the Kokoro-FastAPI image before sign-off.
- (assumption) Proposals, continuity, Rolling Recap and NPC first-token targets hold on the spike evidence, so no reset is proposed. Each holds only under a condition: Ollama has no CPU cap without a matching num_thread, since the old `cpus: "3.0"` gives 0.7 tok/s. The Rolling Recap update is not left queued behind an extraction (E0-LLM.6). The NPC prompt stays at or under about 1,500 tokens (E6). E0-LLM.6 may still propose values for these rows here.
