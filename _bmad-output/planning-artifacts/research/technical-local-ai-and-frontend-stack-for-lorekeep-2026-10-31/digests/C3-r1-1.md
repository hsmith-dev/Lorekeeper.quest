## Tentative recommendation

**STT:** faster-whisper (CTranslate2 int8, "small" or "base.en"/"small.en") behind a WhisperLive- or WhisperLiveKit-style WebSocket server. Use the LocalAgreement-2 policy with Silero VAC and 1-2 s min chunk, and have the browser send AudioWorklet PCM16 at 16 kHz mono (avoids FFmpeg/Opus decode). It reuses the existing Whisper stack.
- Runner-up: whisper.cpp (MIT, smallest RAM, actively released; the custom WebSocket wrapper is ours to write).
- Watch: Parakeet-v3 via onnx-asr/sherpa-onnx (CC-BY-4.0, RTFx 36 on a fast desktop CPU, strong WER, but chunked rather than true streaming and ≥2 GB RAM).
- RAM/CPU cost: small int8 is about 1.5 GB RAM, with batch transcription about 6-8x faster than real time on an 8-thread desktop (derived; audio length unverified). base is roughly half that.
- End-to-end latency: about 2-4 s is plausible on CPU (3.3 s was GPU-measured, so this is unverified for CPU).
- License: MIT code plus MIT Whisper weights (Whisper weight license not fetched this run; belief). Compatible with GPL plus commercial.

**Diarization:** offline post-session with pyannote community-1 (MIT code, CC-BY-4.0 weights, so attribution is required). Weights are gated, so the self-hoster must accept terms or we instruct a one-time HF download; we cannot simply redistribute without honoring the gating terms (verify).
- Runner-up: sherpa-onnx offline diarization (pyannote-seg-3.0 ONNX plus 3D-Speaker/WeSpeaker embeddings), with lighter dependencies and no torch.
- Live diarization is not recommended: Sortformer is capped at 4 speakers and its numbers are GPU-only, and diart is weakly maintained and needs gated models. Tabletop tables often have 4-6 people.
- RAM/CPU cost: unknown; no CPU RTF was found. Benchmark locally before committing.

## Leads
- WhisperLiveKit is the closest off-the-shelf match (browser AudioWorklet, LocalAgreement/SimulStreaming, diarization, CPU Docker). Check its CPU latency issues and dependency weight.
- The faster-whisper release gap (no release since 2025-10) is worth watching for CTranslate2/Python-version breakage; check the issue tracker.
- Moonshine streaming models (MIT) may beat Whisper base/small on CPU latency. Fetch the LICENSE file and HF model cards for WER/latency.
- sherpa-onnx streaming Zipformer/Parakeet models, and their speaker-diarization docs page (the URL moved to k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/speaker-diarization/), may have RTF numbers.
- The Nemotron-3-Diarization model (8 speakers) is mentioned on the Sortformer card. Check its license and CPU viability.
- NeMo-Speech.cpp (GGUF Parakeet/Sortformer on CPU) could be a torch-free path.
- Distil-Whisper distil-small.en / distil-large-v3.5: not fetched this run.
