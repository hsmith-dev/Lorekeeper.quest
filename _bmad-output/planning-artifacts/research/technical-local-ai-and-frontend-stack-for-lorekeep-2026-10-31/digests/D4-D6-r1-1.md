### Quality evidence
- TTS Arena V2 (HF Space TTS-AGI/TTS-Arena-V2) exists and is running, but the leaderboard data was not retrievable by fetch | https://huggingface.co/spaces/TTS-AGI/TTS-Arena-V2 | HF | unknown | accessed 2026-10-31 | low | performance

Tentative recommendation (D4): Kokoro-82M through the ONNX or Kokoro-FastAPI CPU image. Weights are Apache-2.0, misaki is Apache-2.0, and there are 54 voices with weighted blending for genre narrators. Cost: about 300 MB model (about 80 MB quantized); RAM is estimated under 1 GB but not verified; CPU first-chunk latency is about 1-3.5 s. License caveat: avoid or isolate the espeak-ng fallback (GPL-3.0 by belief, not verified this run) in the commercial edition. Options: misaki dictionary-only mode, a separate-process espeak binary (legal review needed), or accept it in the GPL edition only.
Runner-up: Chatterbox Nano (110M, MIT code, 3x realtime on 8 cores, expressive tags, cloning to make custom narrators). Its weights license still needs confirming, and all output carries the Perth watermark.
Exclude for commercial: XTTS-v2 (CPML non-commercial, vendor defunct), F5-TTS (CC-BY-NC), piper1-gpl (GPL-3.0; the GPL edition is OK, but the commercial edition is blocked unless the old MIT rhasspy/piper is pinned, and that one is archived and also uses espeak-ng phonemization, so verify how it links espeak-ng).

Tentative recommendation (D6): use a single semantic HTML+CSS source per chronicle. Render the PDF with WeasyPrint (BSD; adds Pango/HarfBuzz system libs, estimated at tens of MB but not measured). Build the EPUB3 by writing the zip (mimetype, container.xml, OPF, nav.xhtml, XHTML chapters) directly with the stdlib zipfile module. That is a small amount of code and avoids AGPL EbookLib. CPU cost is light; RAM grows with page and image count (not measured).
Runner-up: Typst via typst-py (Apache-2.0, about 30 MB wheel, no system deps, strong typography). It needs a separate template language and gives no EPUB path, so it still needs the hand-rolled EPUB writer.
Avoid: EbookLib (AGPL) in the commercial edition. Pandoc (GPL, plus large image) is acceptable only if invoked as a separate binary after legal review; not recommended.

Leads
- WeasyPrint docs pages for @page margin boxes, string-set, target-counter, PDF/A, PDF/UA.
- EPUBCheck in CI for EPUB3 validity.
- Measure the added Docker MB for WeasyPrint deps on python:3.12-slim.
- MIT/BSD EPUB writers (e.g. a pypub alternative) and their maintenance.
- ReportLab (BSD open-source core) not screened.
